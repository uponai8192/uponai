# UponAI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UponAI ("UponAI," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit uponai.com, use our applications, dashboards, APIs, voice agents, messaging tools, analytics, billing tools, documentation, or related services (collectively, the "Services").

This Privacy Policy is intended for a general business audience. Certain customers may have separate written agreements with UponAI that govern business-to-business services, data processing, security, or compliance obligations. If there is a conflict between this Privacy Policy and a signed agreement, the signed agreement controls to the extent of the conflict.

## 1. Personal Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal information, depending on how you interact with the Services.

### Account and Contact Information

This may include name, business email address, phone number, company name, job title, username, password or authentication information, workspace membership, account role, and preferences.

### Customer and End-User Communications

When customers use the Services to operate AI voice agents, messaging, support, sales, or workflow tools, we may process call details, caller or recipient phone numbers, call audio, recordings, transcripts, messages, routing metadata, agent responses, notes, summaries, call outcomes, and related communications data.

### Usage and Device Information

We may collect log data, IP address, browser type, device identifiers, operating system, pages viewed, referring URLs, session data, timestamps, feature usage, clicks, diagnostics, error logs, and performance data.

### Billing and Transaction Information

We may collect billing contact details, plan information, invoices, payment status, usage charges, tax information, accounting identifiers, and transaction records. Payment card or bank details may be processed by third-party payment processors rather than stored directly by UponAI.

### Integration Data

If you connect third-party services, we may process information from or about those services, such as CRM records, accounting customer IDs, QuickBooks data, support tickets, telephony provider data, calendar data, contact data, messaging data, API keys, access tokens, and configuration settings.

### Support and Feedback

We may collect information you provide through support requests, emails, chat, forms, surveys, demos, calls, bug reports, and feedback.

### Cookies and Similar Technologies

We may use cookies, local storage, pixels, analytics tools, and similar technologies to operate the Services, remember preferences, improve performance, detect fraud, measure usage, and support marketing or analytics.

With your consent, we also use non-essential cookies that enable analytics and lead-attribution providers. These third-party services help us measure site engagement and identify business interest in our products. You can accept or decline these non-essential cookies at any time using the cookie preferences control on our site, and declining will not affect your ability to use the website.

The Services may use artificial intelligence, machine learning, transcription, speech, language, and automation technologies to provide features such as voice agents, call summaries, classifications, recommendations, quality review, routing, and analytics.

We process Customer Data and personal information through AI systems as needed to provide enabled features. Customers are responsible for configuring their agents and workflows, obtaining required consents, and determining whether AI features are appropriate for their use case.
